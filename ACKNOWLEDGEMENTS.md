# PRAMANA — Acknowledgements & Citations

The **PRAMANA** system (from Sanskrit *pramāṇa* [प्रमाण]: "proof, means of knowledge, accurate perception") synthesizes cognitive science methodologies, stateful multi-step agent orchestration, cryptographic auditability, and deterministic validation.

We gratefully acknowledge the researchers, foundational libraries, and open-source ecosystems that made this architecture possible:

---

### 1. Cognitive Psychology & Cognitive Interview Methodology
- **Geiselman, R. E., Fisher, R. P., MacKinnon, D. P., & Holland, H. L. (1985)**: *Eyewitness memory enhancement in the police interview: Cognitive retrieval mnemonics versus hypnosis.* Journal of Applied Psychology, 70(2), 401–412.
- **Fisher, R. P., & Geiselman, R. E. (1992)**: *Memory-enhancing techniques for investigative interviewing: The cognitive interview.* Charles C Thomas, Publisher.
- **DeepMind Cognitive Modeling & Truth-Seeking Inquiries**: Cognitive frameworks inspiring non-leading cognitive interviewing protocols, unbiased memory elicitation, and avoidance of presupposition tags in agent questioning.

---

### 2. Agent Frameworks & Orchestration
- **LangChain & LangGraph.js (`@langchain/langgraph`)**: Stateful, cyclical graph runtime that orchestrates atomic claim extraction, deterministic slot gating, and conditional human-in-the-loop clarification routing.
- **Groq Technologies (`@langchain/groq`)**: Ultra-low-latency LPU inference powering the `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` model for JSON-constrained atomic claim decomposition and cognitive question synthesis.

---

### 3. Data Infrastructure & Cryptographic Integrity
- **Supabase PostgreSQL (`@supabase/supabase-js`)**: Relational storage providing transactional foreign key cascades and relational integrity across cases, statements, claims, and findings.
- **Node.js Crypto API**: Deterministic SHA-256 hash-chaining inspired by blockchain append-only ledgers to provide tamper-evident cryptographic provenance for narrative statements.

---

### 4. Interactive Visualization & Design System
- **React Flow (`@xyflow/react`)**: Interactive, customizable node-based canvas for rendering claim, evidence, and conflict relationship graphs.
- **Tailwind CSS & shadcn/ui**: High-performance, accessible styling system for dark-mode forensic dashboards.
- **Lucide Icons (`lucide-react`)**: Clean, consistent glyphs and iconography.
